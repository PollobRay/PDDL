Color the map of Australia (see example.png) using three colors: red, green and blue.
No two adjacent regions may have the same color.

Regions:
    WA  - Western Australia
    NT  - Northern Territory
    SA  - South Australia
    Q   - Queensland
    NSW - New South Wales
    V   - Victoria
    T   - Tasmania

Adjacent regions:
    WA  : NT, SA
    NT  : WA, SA, Q
    SA  : WA, NT, Q, NSW, V
    Q   : NT, SA, NSW
    NSW : Q, SA, V
    V   : SA, NSW
    T   : (none)

The goal is to color every region.
