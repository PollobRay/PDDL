The Dining Philosophers Problem states that there are 5 philosophers who are engaged in two activities:
thinking and eating.
Meals are taken communally at a table with five plates and five forks arranged in a cycle.
To eat, a philosopher needs both the left and the right fork.
Each fork is shared with a neighbour, so two neighbours cannot eat at the same time.

Forks:
    Ph1 : left F1, right F2
    Ph2 : left F2, right F3
    Ph3 : left F3, right F4
    Ph4 : left F4, right F5
    Ph5 : left F5, right F1

Initially every philosopher is thinking and every fork is on the table.
The goal is that every philosopher has eaten once.
