Problem Description:

    There are three couples (husbands and wives) who want to cross a river using a boat.
    The boat is initially at side1 and can carry one or two people at a time.
    No wife can be in the presence of another man unless her own husband is also present.
    This rule applies on both sides of the river and in the boat, so two people
    can cross together only if they are two men, two women, or a married couple.

The goal is to find a way to get all six people across the river.
