Let,
    the board be a 5x5 chess board.
    The knight is initially at position (1,1).
    The knight has to visit every position on the chess board exactly once.

In the PDDL model a position is written as (row, col) using n1 to n5.
A knight moves 2 rows and 1 column, or 2 columns and 1 row,
and it can never move to a position it has already visited.
