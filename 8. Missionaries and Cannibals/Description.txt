In this problem, three missionaries and three cannibals have to cross the river from side1 to side2.
The boat is initially at side1.

Conditions:
- The boat can carry either one or two people, and it cannot cross empty.
- The goal is to move all missionaries and cannibals
    from one side of the river to the other without ever leaving
    more cannibals than missionaries on either side (unless there are no missionaries on that side).
