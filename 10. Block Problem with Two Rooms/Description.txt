There are 2 rooms with 1 large table each, 6 blocks, and 1 robot gripper.
The robot can pick up (or put down) one block at a time, and can move from one room
to another holding at most 1 block. The robot starts in room1 (Room-Left).
(The original question is in "Problem Description.jpg".)

Operators:
    pick-up(?obj ?from ?room)  : pick up ?obj from the block/table ?from in room ?room
    put-down(?obj ?to ?room)   : put down ?obj onto the block/table ?to in room ?room
    move(?from_room ?to_room)  : move the robot from ?from_room to ?to_room

Initial state:
    room1 (Table1):  A on B, C           room2 (Table2):  D, F on E

        A                                      F
        B   C                              D   E
    ------------                       ------------

Goal state:
    room1 (Table1):  D on E on F         room2 (Table2):  A on B on C

        D                                      A
        E                                      B
        F                                      C
    ------------                       ------------
